# ProPresenter 7 Standard Operating Guide This document outlines the standardized workflow, automation timelines, and Look configurations for the VCBA Media production environment. --- ## 1. Master Sunday Playlist Timeline We have consolidated our workflow down to a single master template: **`REGULAR_SUNDAY_TEMPLATE`**. This replaces all previous variations (such as separate First Sunday templates) to ensure an absolute linear flow. ### 📋 Linear Service Flow * **PRE-SERVICE & COUNTDOWN** — Automated rolling loops and video countdowns. * **WORSHIP** — The core song selection pulled straight from the standard `Song library`. * **ANNOUNCEMENT & T&O** — Tithing, offering segments, and active church life media. * **SERMON** — The active series presentation package followed by the Benediction. * **POST-SERVICE** — Automated exit loops. ### 🍞 First Sunday Communion Protocol On the first Sunday of the month, do not switch playlists or alter the master template structure: 1. Select the **`VCBAMEDIA`** folder under the Library panel. 2. Locate the **Communion** presentation asset. 3. Drag and drop it directly into the active `REGULAR_SUNDAY_TEMPLATE` playlist under the **WORSHIP** or **ANNOUNCEMENT & T&O** header. 4. *Post-Service:* Right-click the Communion item and remove it from the playlist to keep the template clean for the following week. --- ## 2. Calendar & Timeline Automation Pre-service transitions are completely automated using ProPresenter’s Calendar Scheduler. The operator does not need to manually trigger announcements, the stream, or the countdown. ### 📍 How to Open the Calendar Window Because the default keyboard shortcut (`Ctrl + Alt + C`) often conflicts with Windows system graphics hotkeys and may not trigger, always use the application menu: 1. Go to the top menu bar and click **View**. 2. Scroll down and click **Calendar**. ### Master Sunday Timeline Reference | Event Name | Scheduled Time | Triggered Action / Playlist Item | Purpose | | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | | **Pre-Service Loop** | 9:40:00 AM | `Announcement Loop` Presentation | Automatically fires rolling announcement graphics to house screens. | | **Start Livestream** | 9:50:00 AM | `Start Streaming` Action Command | *Automated Go-Live.* Instructs ProPresenter to output the active RTMP stream. | | **5 Min Countdown** | 9:55:00 AM | `5 Min Countdown` Video / Timer | Switches the presentation to the official 5-minute countdown video. | > ⚠️ **Critical Automation Notes for Operators:** > * **Calendar Status:** The `Calendar Enabled` checkbox in the top-left of the Calendar settings must always remain checked. Disabling this turns off all automated service transitions. > * **Testing New Events:** When creating separate event items, always ensure the **repeat rule** and **ends on** dates match the exact schedule of that specific event so it does not loop or conflict with the standard Sunday morning master timeline. --- ## 3. Media Bin Background Standards To maintain clear visual separation and reduce the chance of an operator selecting the wrong asset mid-service, the Media Bin utilizes dedicated background playlists: * **🖼️ Worship Background:** Reserved strictly for abstract motion loops, ambient pads, and song lyrics presentation layers. * **📖 Verse Background:** Reserved specifically for sermon aesthetic packages, scripture reading backdrops, and sermon series graphic templates.
