⚠️ The Hidden "Homework Tax" & Contract Realities

When migrating to a brand-new system, the true cost isn't just the software invoice. Based on Ryan's discussion with the Subsplash sales representative, the platform relies on restrictive contract gates: to drop their baseline monthly cost to the discounted tier, they legally force the church into a strict 24-month commitment and demand a massive, 100% upfront annual lump-sum check. If the platform creates major database silos or accounting friction for Joel, we lose all leverage to exit.

Typically operates via Net Settlement (processing fees are deducted before the money hits our bank account). This will require Joel to completely rebuild our weekly bank reconciliation process.

Core Expenses Included:

Subsplash Base Contract ($366.99/mo) + PCO Services ($69/mo) + ProPresenter 7 ($30/mo) | Restream/Bluehost discontinued

Core Pros & Cons:
  • All-In-One Frontend: Replaces Bluehost (fixing our site) and replaces Restream (built-in live media player).
  • Beautiful media archives and unified outward church communication.
  • Split Database Burden: Because we keep Planning Center Services for Saturday night team scheduling and prep, Subsplash fragments our database. Joel/Tech must manage separate silos.
  • Requires an upfront Standard Onboarding Fee ($999) reviewed during the sales call.

Aggregated Community Reviews for Subsplash

The Good (The Congregant View)

Churches uniformly praise the frontend interface. The custom app builder is smooth, and the media storage layout handles sermon audio, video, and streaming wonderfully. For communities prioritizing outward engagement and a premium media footprint, it receives exceptionally high marks.

The Bad (The Administrative View)

Admin staff frequently note that Subsplash is a "walled garden." Its internal church database profiles are shallow compared to Planning Center. If a church uses external systems for complex team scheduling or check-ins, Subsplash does not "play nice," forcing manual data entry or CSV syncs to keep profiles updated.
