🚨 4. Critical Discovery: The Subsplash Onboarding Trap & Feature Assessment
⚠️ The $999 vs. $5,000 Real Cost Breakdown

During deep file verification, we uncovered a hidden barrier in Subsplash's pricing model. The heavily marketed $999 package is exclusively a baseline structural coaching fee. It does not buy you an outsourced build.

• The $999 "Standard" Tier: Subsplash sets up your legal Apple/Google Developer wrappers, but leaves 100% of the graphic design, data migration, page building, and calendar setups on our desks as a manual homework task.
• The $5,000 "Comprehensive" Tier: If we want them to actively migrate our data, build out content structures, and handle the layout configuration for us, they shift the church into a luxury enterprise contract.

💡 Why Subsplash Looks Good on Paper — But Fails Our Ministry

Subsplash pitches itself to smaller churches by offering slightly lower credit card rates (2.3% vs. Pushpay's 2.9%). However, for a congregation of ~250 people, Planning Center completely destroys both paradigms. PCO Giving has a completely free base tier ($0/mo) and charges a flat $0.25 fee for bank ACH transfers. Spending $5,000 upfront or paying a massive $459 text-and-app premium just to get slightly cheaper card transaction rates is a losing strategy for VCBA's resources.
