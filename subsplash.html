🚨 3. Critical Discovery: The Subsplash Onboarding Trap
⚠️ The $999 vs. $5,000 Real Cost Breakdown

During deep file verification, we uncovered a hidden barrier in Subsplash's pricing model. The heavily marketed $999 package is exclusively a baseline structural coaching fee. It does not buy you an outsourced build.

• The $999 "Standard" Tier: Subsplash sets up wrappers, but leaves 100% of design, data migration, page building, and calendar configurations on our desks as a manual task.
• The $5,000 "Comprehensive" Tier: Shifting content over and layout data architecture shifts us directly into a custom corporate enterprise contract.

💡 Why Subsplash Looks Good on Paper — But Fails Our Ministry

Subsplash pitches lower credit card fees (2.3% vs. Pushpay's 2.9%). However, for a church of ~250 people, Planning Center destroys both paradigms. PCO Giving has a $0/mo base tier and flat $0.25 bank ACH transfers. Paying a high premium to drop merchant rates is a net resource loss for VCBA.
