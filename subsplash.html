VCBA — Tech & Budget Evaluation Dashboard

Analyzing administrative labor, platform overlap, and financial stewardship.
BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front)

Subsplash forces a massive manual project onto our team for a $999 coaching fee, rather than building the app for us. Shifting completely to Planning Center (PCO) paid modules unifies our community workflow into one single app, overcomes free tier limits, and still saves the church $2,220 every single year to invest in help instead of software platforms.

Subsplash

Verdict: No-Go
$999 framework fee
  • Not a Custom Build: The $999 tier is just for coaching. They don't actually build out the pages or structure for us.
  • Extra Homework: Turns into a massive administrative project where our team has to manually build the entire app from scratch.
  • High Premium: High upfront cost without saving our team any actual technical labor.

Pushpay

Verdict: Status Quo
$399/month bill
  • Functional but Split: Works fine for basic needs, but leaves our community split across two different applications.
  • High Cost: Keeps the church locked into a high recurring monthly payment layout.

Planning Center (PCO)

Verdict: Best Route
Saves $2,220/year
  • One Single App: Unifies giving, events, and groups directly inside Church Center. We are explicitly not using kids check-in yet.
  • Low Learning Curve: Since some folks are already playing around with check-ins and registrations, learning it won't be bad.
  • Free Tier Bottleneck: The historical pushback we felt was simply because we used the free tier instead of paid modules. Unlocking paid tiers fixes the issue completely.
💡 Budget Strategy: People Over Platforms

Instead of chasing a "perfect" software platform, keeping our tech costs low with Planning Center saves us $2,220 every single year. We can use that exact saved budget to hire a part-time church youth or a basic admin assistant to do the weekly data entry, app updates, and website text updates for the office. This keeps our leadership team entirely focused on strategy instead of getting bogged down in weekly administrative desk work.
