Strategic Platform Assessment

An operational overview of technology options for VCBA, June 2026.

Option 1: Pushpay + PCO

Maintains existing financial tools while utilizing Planning Center (PCO) for scheduling and check-in.

Option 2: Unified PCO

Consolidates giving, database, and scheduling into the Planning Center ecosystem.

Option 3: Subsplash

Provides a custom-branded mobile app and integrated media syndication.

Operational Considerations

  • Congregation Migration: Options 2 and 3 require users to transition their recurring giving profiles.
  • SMS & Email: Subsplash and PCO both support SMS/email workflows, though execution methods and integrations with existing ChMS data differ.
  • Check-in: PCO offers a mature, widely-integrated check-in system. Subsplash offers a newer check-in module with limited third-party review data available.

Onboarding Tier Comparison (Subsplash)

  • Standard ($999): Provides setup coaching and access to training materials; requires internal team to manage all content assembly and layout.
  • Streamlined/Comprehensive: Higher investment tiers that include expanded assistance for data migration and structural configuration.

Technical Discovery Questions

  • Ownership: Does the church retain full ownership of the app store presence and developer account?
  • SMS/Email: What are the specific costs and delivery limits for mass communication?
  • Check-in: Does the check-in module require a physical signature printout for security?
  • Security: What are the protocols regarding data breach notification and recovery?
  • Trial/Demo: Is there a sandboxed trial version available for technical review?
  • Session Recording: Does the platform allow for local session recording/archiving?
  • Support: Is a dedicated contact number provided for technical emergencies?
