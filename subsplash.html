Strategic Platform Assessment

An operational overview of technology options for VCBA, June 2026.

Option 1: Pushpay + Planning Center

Maintains existing financial tools while utilizing Planning Center for scheduling and check-in.

Option 2: Unified Planning Center

Consolidates giving, database, and scheduling into the full Planning Center suite.

Option 3: Subsplash

Provides a custom-branded mobile app and integrated media syndication.

Estimated Annual Platform Costs

Note: Costs are estimates based on a congregation size of 250 and may vary based on specific feature tiers selected.

Strategic Considerations

  • Church Management Software (CMS): Planning Center offers a highly integrated database that connects giving, check-in, and scheduling. Subsplash acts as an engagement layer but typically requires manual integration to sync data back to the primary database.
  • Congregation Experience: Unified Planning Center uses the "Church Center" app for all functions. Subsplash provides a custom-branded app under the church's name.
  • Onboarding Realities: Options involving a switch in payment processors require a transition period where donors must update their recurring giving profiles.

Technical Discovery Questions

  • App Store Ownership: Does the church retain full ownership of the app store presence and developer account?
  • Communication (SMS/Email): What are the specific costs and delivery limits for mass communication tools?
  • Check-in Security: Does the check-in module require physical signature printouts for security?
  • Data Security: What are the specific protocols regarding data breach notification and recovery?
  • Demonstration: Is there a sandboxed trial version available for technical review?
  • Media/Streaming: Does the platform allow for local session recording and archiving?
  • Support: Is a dedicated contact number provided for technical emergencies?
